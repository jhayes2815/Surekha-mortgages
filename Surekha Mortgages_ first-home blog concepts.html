Stamp Duty relief

In England and Northern Ireland, first-time buyers pay nothing up to £300,000, and relief ends above £500,000.

Four nations, four rulebooks

Scotland uses LBTT and Wales uses LTT, each with its own thresholds. Scotland also has offers-over sales and Home Reports.
